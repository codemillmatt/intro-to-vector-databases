# The Cyber Siege

## Chapter 1

The story begins exactly where it needs to: with a quiet moment that refuses to stay quiet. What seems ordinary at first slowly reveals the tension beneath it, shaped by the forces already at work in this world.

## Chapter 2

Events accelerate as consequences appear. Choices made earlier now demand payment, and the cost is higher than anyone expected. The themes hinted at before begin to take shape through action rather than explanation.

## Chapter 3

Conflict reaches its peak. What was once uncertain becomes unavoidable. The characters confront the truth of their situation and their role within it, whether they are ready or not.

## Chapter 4

Resolution does not come easily, but it comes honestly. The ending leaves the world changed—sometimes subtly, sometimes forever—by what has occurred.
