🔍 How These Recommendations Work

Content-Based Filtering

Creates a "preference vector" by averaging the embeddings of your liked books, then finds books with the highest cosine similarity to this preference profile. This captures semantic relationships between book content.

Collaborative Filtering

Identifies users who liked the same books as you, weighted by how many books you share. Then recommends books those similar users enjoyed that you haven't read yet. More shared likes = higher influence.
